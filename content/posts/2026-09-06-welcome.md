Hello! This is my blog. I will write here about the things I make, the books I read and the ideas I have.

## How a post works

Every post is a text file in the `content/posts` folder. The top of the file says the title, the date and the tags. The rest is the story, written in Markdown, which means:

- a line starting with `#` is a heading,
- `*words*` come out *slanted* and `**words**` come out **bold**,
- a line starting with `-` is a bullet like this one.

When a new post is saved and pushed, the site rebuilds itself and the post shows up on the home page.

## What you will find here

I like to draw and to make things with paper, so many posts will have pictures. The [gallery](#/gallery) page collects all of the pictures in one place. The [about](#/about) page says a little about me.

Thank you for reading.
