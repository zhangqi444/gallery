On Sunday we folded paper boats from old magazine pages. I folded three and my dad folded three. His were neater, but mine had better colours.

## The race

We put them in the pond at the same time and blew on them to make wind. Two boats got stuck in the reeds right away. One turned over. The blue one went the farthest, and it was one of mine.

> A boat that floats is a good boat. A boat that goes somewhere is a great boat.

## What I learned

- Thick paper floats longer than thin paper.
- A little bit of crayon on the bottom keeps the water out for a while.
- Ducks are not interested in paper boats at all.

Next time I want to try a boat with a sail.
