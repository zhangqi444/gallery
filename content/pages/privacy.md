title: Privacy
updated: 2026-09-08
---

The Little Me has no server of its own. It keeps nothing about you, because it
has nowhere to keep it.

**Signing in.** Signing in with Google lets this site make files in your own
Google Drive and open the files it made. That is the whole of the permission it
asks for. It cannot see, open or list anything else in your Drive, including
files another app made.

**Where your work lives.** Everything you write here — your practice, your
hours, your pictures and your posts — is saved in a folder of your own Google
Drive, under your own Google account. It is never copied to a server belonging
to this site, because there is no such server. Nobody running this site can read
it.

**What ever leaves your Drive.** Nothing, until you press Publish on a blog.
Publishing shares that one file so anyone with the link can read it.
Unpublishing takes the sharing away. Deleting a post deletes its file, and the
pictures it used, from your Drive.

**What your browser keeps.** The app also keeps a copy of your work in your
browser, so it opens at once and works without a connection. Clearing this
site's data in your browser removes that copy and leaves the one in your Drive
alone.

**What is collected.** Nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no
tracking, and no account with anybody but Google.

**Taking it back.** Sign out whenever you like. To withdraw the app's permission
altogether, remove it at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
Your files stay in your Drive, yours to keep or delete like any others.
