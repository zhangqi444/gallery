My hobby is drawing. I draw almost every day, usually after school and sometimes at breakfast.

## What I draw

Mostly animals. Cats are the easiest. Horses are the hardest, because of the legs. Sometimes I draw houses with lots of windows, and once I drew a whole city on a piece of paper as long as the kitchen table.

## What I use

- Coloured pencils, which live in a jar on my desk.
- A black felt pen for the outlines.
- Ordinary printer paper, because there is always more of it.

## Why I like it

When I draw I stop thinking about everything else. And at the end there is a picture that was not there before.
