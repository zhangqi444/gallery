I kept a list of every book I finished this summer. There were eleven, and I read one of them twice because I forgot the ending and wanted to know it again.

## The four I would tell a friend about

1. **A book about a lighthouse.** It was slow at first, and then I could not stop.
2. **A book about a girl who talks to crows.** The crows were the best part.
3. **A comic about the ocean.** I learned that some jellyfish can glow.
4. **A book of short poems.** I read one every night before bed.

## The plan for autumn

I want to read one long book, slowly, a chapter a night. I have not chosen it yet. If you have an idea, tell me.
